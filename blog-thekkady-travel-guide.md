# Thekkady Wed–Fri: Your Ultimate 3-Day Guide (May 27–29, 2026)

> **Weather heads-up before anything else:** The IMD declared the 2026 southwest monsoon onset over Kerala on **May 24** — three days before you arrive. Expect warm, humid days around 25°C, lush green forests, moody mist over the Periyar Lake, and afternoon/evening showers. Pack a lightweight rain jacket, quick-dry clothes, and closed-toe shoes. The silver lining? Thekkady in early monsoon is genuinely beautiful, crowds are thin, prices are lower, and the wildlife refuge is at its greenest.

---

## Should I Stay at Poetree Thekkady?

**Yes — strongly recommend it.**

The resort is officially called **WGH Poetree Thekkady** (previously Poetree Sarovar Portico), perched on a hilltop at Ottakathalamedu with sweeping views of the Sahyadri Mountains and Periyar Lake. Here's why it works especially well for a Wed–Fri May trip:

- **Infinity pool with valley views** — stunning in soft monsoon light
- **Rooms are spacious and very clean** — recent 2025–2026 guest reviews consistently highlight this
- **On-site activities** — yoga, guided nature walks, two restaurants, and cultural performances mean you don't need to scramble for things to do if rain pins you in
- **Warm, attentive staff** — one of the most praised aspects across every platform
- **Value** — at roughly $89–$111/night, it delivers a premium hilltop experience at a mid-range price

**One note:** The Poetree is not in Thekkady town itself — it's about 15–20 minutes away, sitting in the hills. That's a plus for atmosphere, but means you'll need their shuttle or a hire car for the Periyar Sanctuary and town activities. Factor that in.

### What about "Thekkady Gavi Suites"?
There's a separate 3-star property called **Thekkady Gavi Suites** (near Vandiperiyar, on the Kottayam–Kumily Road) — budget-friendly (~$20–$32/night), clean and well-reviewed for what it is. Fine if you're purely using the hotel as a base and budget matters. But for a short 3-day escape, Poetree's atmosphere wins comfortably.

---

## Should I Go to Gavi Safari?

**Go — but adjust your expectations for May monsoon.**

Gavi is a gorgeous eco-tourism enclave about 1 hour from Thekkady inside the Periyar Tiger Reserve. In dry season (Oct–Feb) it's world-class. In late May with the monsoon just hitting, here's the honest picture:

| Factor | Reality in Late May |
|---|---|
| **Trails** | Wet and slippery — trekking is harder |
| **Wildlife** | Animals retreat deeper into the forest during rain |
| **Greenery** | Absolutely stunning — forest at peak lushness |
| **Crowds** | Very low — you'll almost have it to yourself |
| **Jeep safari** | Usually still running — confirm booking day before |
| **Boat on Gavi Lake** | Excellent even in light rain |

**Book in advance** at the official KFDC ecotourism site (kfdcecotourism.com) — entry is capped daily and they turn people away at the gate without a reservation. Carry a printed or digital copy, your vehicle registration, and a photo ID for every passenger.

**Verdict:** If you're a nature lover, the early monsoon forest is genuinely special — deep green, misty, near-silent. Just don't expect the big cat or large mammal sightings you'd get in November. Go for the forest atmosphere, the lake, and the birds (260+ species recorded).

## Your 3-Day Itinerary

### Wednesday, May 27 — Arrival & Settle In

- **Afternoon:** Check in to Poetree Thekkady. Take the hilltop views in from your room. If it's drizzling, that's already the postcard moment.
- **Evening:** Dip in the infinity pool (rain or shine — it's warm water, moody sky). Freshen up.
- **Night:** Dinner at Poetree's on-site restaurant — they do solid Indian cuisine. Ask staff about the Kathakali or Kalaripayattu performance schedule (often held nearby in Kumily town in the evenings).
- **Pro tip:** Book your Periyar Lake boat ride for Thursday morning before you sleep tonight — slots fill up even in off-season.

---

### Thursday, May 28 — Periyar Lake & Spice Country

- **Early morning (6:30–7am):** Head to **Periyar Lake** for the morning boat cruise. This is the best wildlife-watching window of the day. Elephants, deer, otters, and herons come to the lakeside at dawn. The forest mist on the water in early monsoon is extraordinary.
- **Morning:** After the cruise, take a **guided spice plantation tour** — Thekkady is cardamom and black pepper country. Most resorts can arrange this. You'll walk through cardamom groves, pepper vines, and nutmeg trees. Buy direct from the farm; it's significantly cheaper and fresher.
- **Afternoon:** Rest back at the resort — monsoon afternoons often bring heavier showers. This is prime time for an **Ayurvedic massage** (book at the resort or nearby; Kerala monsoon is considered the ideal season for Ayurveda — humidity helps herbal oils absorb).
- **Evening:** Catch a **Kathakali performance** in Kumily town. Most cultural centres run shows at 5–6pm. It's theatrical, colourful, and takes about 90 minutes. Worth it even if you don't know the story — the makeup and drumming alone are absorbing.

---

### Friday, May 29 — Gavi Safari & Departure

- **Very early start (5:30–6am):** Drive to Gavi — the 1-hour journey through the hills at dawn is stunning in itself. You need to reach the check post before 7am for morning entry.
- **Morning in Gavi:** Jeep safari through the forest, boating on Gavi Lake, and quiet time at the viewpoints. Pack insect repellent, a rain layer, and binoculars if you have them — birding here is exceptional even in monsoon.
- **Midday:** Head back to Thekkady (by 12–1pm).
- **Afternoon:** Check out of Poetree. If time allows, browse the spice shops in **Kumily Market** on the way out — good prices on cardamom, vanilla, and pepper.

## Practical Notes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Getting around** | Hire a local taxi/auto for the trip (~₹800–1,200/day) or use the resort shuttle — public transport to Gavi doesn't exist |
| **What to pack** | Lightweight rain jacket, closed-toe shoes, insect repellent, sunscreen, power bank |
| **Book ahead** | Gavi entry pass (kfdcecotourism.com), Periyar boat ride (periyartigerreserve.org), Kathakali show |
| **Carry cash** | Gavi entry, plantation tips, and local markets often don't take cards |
| **Mobile signal** | Patchy inside Gavi forest — download offline maps before you go |
| **Best time of day** | Early mornings for wildlife; afternoons for rest/Ayurveda; evenings for culture |

---

## The Bottom Line

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Stay at Poetree Thekkady? | **Yes.** Hilltop views, great pool, warm staff, solid value. |
| Go to Gavi Safari? | **Yes — with adjusted expectations.** Book in advance, go early, embrace the monsoon forest. |
| Is late May/early monsoon OK? | **Yes — with the right mindset.** Green, misty, uncrowded, atmospheric. Not ideal for wildlife counts, but genuinely beautiful. |

Three days is just enough to hit the highlights without rushing. Wednesday arrival, Thursday for Periyar and culture, Friday for Gavi — that's a clean, satisfying loop.
